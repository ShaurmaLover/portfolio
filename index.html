My services

Frontend Development

Frontend development involves building the visual and interactive parts of a website that users see and interact with, using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It ensures a website is responsive and works well on different devices and browsers.

Working with your design

I can bring your design to life by converting your mockups or design files into fully functional websites. Whether you have a complete design or just a concept, I will ensure that it’s translated accurately into code, preserving the look and feel while making it interactive, responsive, and user-friendly.

Figma, Wix, Wordpress and etc. layouts

I work with a variety of design tools and platforms, including Figma, Wix, and WordPress, to bring your vision to life. Whether you have a custom design in Figma, need a quick start with a Wix template, or want to build a WordPress site.

Responsive Design

I create websites that look great on any device, whether it's a desktop, tablet, or smartphone. Using responsive design techniques, I ensure your site adapts to different screen sizes, providing a seamless and user-friendly experience across all devices.

Adding Forms with Email Submission

I can integrate contact forms on your website that allow users to submit inquiries directly to your email. Whether it's a simple contact form or a more complex one, I ensure smooth functionality, making it easy for you to receive messages and interact with your visitors.

Animations

I can add engaging animations to your website to make it more interactive and visually appealing. Using libraries like AOS, ScrollMagic, and custom JavaScript, I bring elements to life with smooth transitions, scrolling effects, and other dynamic features to enhance the user experience.
